Secret Santa

How it works
  1. Find your name below and tap it.
  2. Make up any 4 numbers (like 1225). This is your PIN.
  3. Tap “Draw my name.” You’ll see who you’re buying for and what they want.
  4. Type your own wish list so whoever got you knows what you like.
  5. Keep it a secret! Bring your gift to the Christmas Eve party.
Want to look again or change your wish list? Tap your name and type your 4 numbers.
