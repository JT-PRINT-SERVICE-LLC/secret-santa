Secret Santa

How it works
  1. Tap your name below.
  2. Type your wish list so your Secret Santa knows what you like.
  3. Make up any 4 numbers (like 1225). That’s your PIN.
  4. Spin the wheel! It lands on who you’re buying for and shows their wish list.
  5. Keep it a secret and bring your gift to the Christmas Eve party.
Come back anytime: tap your name and type your PIN to see your person again or change your wish list.
